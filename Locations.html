How to Get There

By Bus

Take a bus from Manila to Baguio City, then transfer to Mountain Province. Buses run regularly from major terminals.

By Car

Drive via the Halsema Highway. The journey takes about 8-10 hours from Manila. Road conditions vary by season.

By Air

Fly to Tuguegarao Airport, then take a van or bus to Mountain Province. Domestic flights available from Manila.
