Welcome to Mountain Province

Mountain Province is a land of breathtaking landscapes, rich indigenous culture, and warm hospitality. Nestled in the Cordillera Mountains of the Philippines, it offers a unique blend of natural wonders and cultural heritage that captivates every visitor.

From the stunning rice terraces to the serene hanging coffins, our province invites you to explore its diverse attractions. Experience the traditional way of life of the Igorot people, hike through lush forests, and witness the harmonious coexistence of nature and culture.

Join us in discovering the hidden gems of Mountain Province and create unforgettable memories in this paradise of the north.

Our Mission

To promote sustainable tourism that preserves the natural beauty and cultural heritage of Mountain Province while providing unforgettable experiences for visitors. We are committed to responsible travel, community empowerment, and environmental conservation.

  • Preserve indigenous cultures and traditions
  • Promote eco-friendly tourism practices
  • Support local communities and economies
  • Protect natural landscapes and biodiversity

Our Values

Respect

We respect the indigenous cultures, traditions, and the environment of Mountain Province.

Sustainability

We commit to eco-friendly practices and sourcing from ethical suppliers.

Community

We believe in empowering local communities and fostering cultural exchange.
