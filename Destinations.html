Explore Our Destinations

Sagada

UNESCO World Heritage Site featuring stunning rice terraces, hanging coffins, and cave explorations. Experience the rich Igorot culture and breathtaking mountain views.

UNESCO Heritage Site
Bauko

Home to the world's highest rice terraces at 2,700 meters above sea level. Discover the engineering marvels of the Igorot people and their sustainable farming practices.

Highest Rice Terraces
Tadian

Known for its pristine mountain landscapes, hot springs, and traditional Igorot villages. Perfect for nature lovers and cultural immersion experiences.

Natural Hot Springs
Sabangan

Known for its rich cultural heritage, stunning natural landscapes, and warm hospitality. Offers a peaceful retreat with rice terraces, weaving centers, and hiking trails.
