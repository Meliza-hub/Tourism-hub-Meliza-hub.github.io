BAUKO

About Bauko

Bauko is home to the world's highest rice terraces at 2,700 meters above sea level. Discover the engineering marvels of the Igorot people and their sustainable farming practices.

Key attractions include the stunning rice terraces, traditional weaving centers, and scenic mountain views.

History of Bauko

Bauko's history is deeply intertwined with the remarkable engineering feats of the Igorot people and their harmonious relationship with the high-altitude environment.

Ancient Engineering

The Igorot people constructed the world's highest rice terraces over 2,000 years ago, demonstrating extraordinary agricultural innovation at 2,700 meters above sea level. These terraces represent a sustainable farming system that has endured for millennia.

Indigenous Heritage

Bauko has preserved rich Igorot traditions, including intricate weaving techniques and communal farming practices. The community maintains strong cultural identity through traditional governance and social structures.

Modern Development

While maintaining cultural traditions, Bauko has embraced sustainable tourism and community-based initiatives. The terraces continue to be actively farmed, supporting local livelihoods and preserving ancestral lands.

Cultural Preservation

UNESCO recognition has brought global attention to Bauko's terraces, leading to conservation efforts that balance tourism development with cultural preservation and environmental protection.

Travel Tips for Bauko

Getting There

  • By Bus: Take a bus from Manila to Baguio (8-10 hours), then transfer to Bauko (4-6 hours via Halsema Highway)
  • By Van: Private vans and shared transportation available from Baguio to Bauko
  • By Air: Fly to Cauayan Airport, then arrange ground transportation to Bauko

Best Time to Visit

Visit during the dry season (December-May) for clear views of the terraces. The cool high-altitude climate makes it pleasant year-round.

What to Bring

  • Warm layers (temperatures can drop at night)
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Sun protection (high altitude sun)
  • Rain gear (weather can change quickly)
  • Modest clothing for cultural sites

Local Etiquette

Respect the high-altitude environment and local customs. Ask permission before photographing people or sacred sites. Support local businesses and guides.

Attractions in Bauko

Rice Terraces

UNESCO World Heritage Site featuring the highest rice terraces in the world at 2,700 meters above sea level. These ancient terraces showcase remarkable Igorot engineering and sustainable farming practices.

Weaving Center

Visit traditional weaving centers to learn about Igorot textile arts and purchase authentic handwoven products. Experience the intricate patterns and cultural significance of indigenous craftsmanship.

Mountain Views

Enjoy breathtaking panoramic views of the Cordillera Mountains and experience the serene highland landscape. Perfect for photography and peaceful contemplation.

Best Time to Visit Bauko

Bauko is ideal to visit during the dry season from December to May when the weather is cool and clear, making it perfect for exploring the rice terraces. The wet season from June to November brings lush greenery but can be challenging for outdoor activities.

Dry Season

December - May

Cool temperatures, clear views

Wet Season

June - November

Lush landscapes, occasional showers

Local Cuisine & Culture

Traditional Igorot Dishes

  • Tinuno: Smoked meat seasoned with herbs and spices
  • Pinakbet: Mixed vegetables with bagoong alamang
  • Etag: Smoked pork or beef, a local specialty
  • Vegetable stews: Fresh mountain vegetables and herbs

Try authentic Igorot cuisine at local eateries and homestays.

Cultural Experiences

Bauko offers deep cultural immersion:

  • Traditional weaving workshops
  • Igorot dance and music performances
  • Visit to indigenous communities
  • Learn about rice terrace farming

Engage respectfully with local traditions and customs.

Accommodation Options

Budget Lodges

Simple accommodations starting from PHP 400/night. Clean and basic facilities with mountain views.

Mid-range Inns

Comfortable stays with amenities, ranging from PHP 1,000-2,500/night.

Homestays

Stay with local families for cultural immersion. Meals included, around PHP 700-1,000/night.
